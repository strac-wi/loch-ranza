# Marine Traffic Sim

An unofficial browser sim of the waters off Knapdale and Kintyre: pilot the double-ended ro-ro ferry MV Loch Ranza across the Sound of Gigha between Tayinloan and Gigha, with Voith Schneider propulsion and two-section folding ramps. A yacht and a fishing boat share the water and keep to the Collision Regulations, and a lights quiz tests you on what vessels show by night and by day.

Or pick the RIB on the start screen: a Gala Viking V500 with a Suzuki 100 hp outboard, starting alongside the Boathouse pontoon at Ardminish, with drills for coming alongside, man overboard and a high-speed slalom. Its handling figures are estimates tuned to published specs, so it is a game, not a training aid.

Play it at https://strac-wi.github.io/loch-ranza/

Loch Sween (RIB only, from a mooring off Achnamara): https://strac-wi.github.io/loch-ranza/#sween, or pick it under Water on the start screen. Its shore, roads and woods are traced from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), GSHHG beyond; hills from Copernicus DEM GLO-30 (© DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014-2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA). The map runs from Achnamara out past Danna, the MacCormaig Isles and Knap to Loch Caolisport, and on down the Sound of Jura to the north of Gigha, where the RIB can make fast alongside the Ardminish pontoon (about 23 nautical miles by water), passing the Twin Beaches at Gigha's north end, modelled from satellite views and photographs and marked on the chart, everything placed by latitude and longitude. The shore, drying flats and depths come from charts: screenshots of the GPS Nautical Charts app (gpsnauticalcharts.com) round the moorings and down the east shore, a photographed Admiralty chart of the Sound of Jura for the rest of the loch, and another of the Sound of Gigha for the north of Gigha, their soundings read by eye; in the open Sound between them and along the Kintyre shore the depths are modelled. Castle Sween is modelled from a satellite view and photographs and marked on the chart. Not for navigation.


Live tide (pick Live under Tide on the start screen): today's tide worked out on your device from the harmonic constants of real tide gauges, from TICON-3 (Hart-Davis, M.G., Dettmering, D. and Seitz, F., 2022, PANGAEA, https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.951610, CC BY 4.0), based on GESLA-3. In the Sound of Gigha it is the Port Ellen gauge's tide (Islay, the nearest gauge on this side of Kintyre). Loch Sween has no gauge: there it is the Tobermory gauge's tide shifted 92 minutes earlier and scaled to 0.41, fitted to a week of published Castle Sween predictions from WillyWeather. Not for navigation.
